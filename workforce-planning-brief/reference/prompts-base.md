# Base prompts (unmodified)

These are the original prompts this process is built on top of, kept verbatim so the delta with the amplified versions in `prompts-amplified.md` stays visible and explainable to the user. Use these as the literal starting point for each phase — the amplification layer wraps around them, it doesn't replace them.

## Scope (Phase 2)

> Draft a "Scope" section for a 3-year strategic workforce plan for [Company]'s "[Initiative]." The focus is on launching [initiative details]. The plan should cover the [departments] departments in [geography]. State that [excluded areas] are excluded.

## Assumptions (Phase 3, sub-step 1)

> Based on the [Company] scenario, generate 5–7 key assumptions about workforce needs, talent availability, and employee reskilling required for [the transformation].

## Constraints (Phase 3, sub-step 2)

> Identify 2–3 major real-world constraints for the [Company] workforce plan. Focus on budget limitations, talent market availability, and timeline pressures.

## Risks (Phase 3, sub-step 3)

> Identify 3–5 major risks for the [Company] workforce plan, focusing on talent competition, change resistance from current employees, and budget limitations.

## Mitigations (Phase 3, sub-step 4)

> For each of the risks you just identified, suggest one practical mitigation strategy.

## Success Criteria (Phase 4)

> Create [3–4] specific, measurable success criteria for the [Company] workforce plan. They should relate to hiring key digital roles, upskilling the current workforce, and the direct impact on [the main launch/initiative].
